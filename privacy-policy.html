Cycle Alarm

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Türkçe
In short: Your alarms and settings stay on your phone. Cycle Alarm doesn't connect to the internet, collect data or share anything with anyone.

Who is responsible

Cycle Alarm is developed by EcropApp. For any question about this notice, write to appecrop@gmail.com.

What information is used?

The alarms you create (names, times, periods, notes, holiday dates), the alarm sound you choose and your app settings (such as theme and tips). This information is stored only on your phone and used only to ring your alarms.

Where does it go?

Nowhere. The app has no internet permission; your information is never sent to, sold to or shared with anyone, including the developer. There are no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools in the app.

Backup

If Android backup is on, your alarms and settings are included in the encrypted device backup in your Google account, so they come back when you switch phones. Google manages this backup and its privacy policy applies. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Permissions and why they're needed

Notifications: to show the ringing alarm and evening reminders. Exact alarms: so the alarm rings on the exact minute. Full screen and lock screen: so the alarm screen opens on your lock screen. Foreground service, wake lock and vibration: so sound and vibration aren't cut off while ringing. Run at startup: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts. No permission is used for anything else.

Exporting and sharing

When you choose to export to a calendar, the next 90 days of the cycle are prepared as a calendar file and passed only to the app you pick. From then on, that app's own privacy rules apply.

Legal basis

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and similar laws such as the GDPR, the developer does not collect, store or process your personal data. The information on your phone is entirely under your control; uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently.

Your rights

You have rights such as learning whether your personal data is processed, requesting information, and asking for correction or deletion. Since the developer holds no data about you, no further action is needed for these requests; you're still welcome to send any question or request to appecrop@gmail.com.

Changes

If this notice is updated, the new version appears in the app and on the page where it's published; significant changes are mentioned in the app's release notes.
